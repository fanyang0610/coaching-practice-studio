# 教练练习室 · Coaching Practice Studio

一个独立、免费的中英双语教练练习空间。带一个自己的议题来 **被教练**，结束时带走一张行动卡。也可以 **练习做教练**，由 AI 扮演来访者，练习结束后收到评分和反馈。

在线版通过邀请链接注册。请在浏览器打开[练习室](https://fanyang0610-coaching-practice-studio.hf.space)登录。使用和隐私说明见[帮助](https://fanyang0610-coaching-practice-studio.hf.space/help?lang=zh)。

A free, independent space for practising coaching in English or Simplified Chinese. Bring a topic to **Be coached** and finish with an action card, or **Practise coaching** with an AI coachee and receive a review. The hosted app is invite-only. Open the [studio](https://fanyang0610-coaching-practice-studio.hf.space) directly to sign in.

Source, local setup and maintainer guides are in the [project repository](https://github.com/fanyang0610/coaching-practice-studio) for collaborators with access.
